## Why HackHaste?

**English:** Five vowels `UIAEO`* on one home hand (the nucleus). `HRSTNP`* on the other (the clusters). **ETAOINSHR** all home, on the *correct sides* - *Expedited Typing Across* **OS***es* *Integrated Natively Salvaging Home Rows*. ~¾ of letters never leave home. Hands alternate on the **CV pulse**; **TH ST NT NS RT RS TR ND** *roll* on one hand. Comma/period on the vowel hand so a word-final **T/N/S** does not same-hand the stop. Apostrophe with that hand `'s`, `n't`). 

**Code:** `7-0`* on the top row, `1-6`* above - numbers without leaving the vowel hand. `-` **`_` **`=` **`+` `/` *on that same strong index.* `()` on *`90`**. Caps is **Control** (Command on Apple); shortcuts **follow the letter**; AltGr **on the letter**. Shift Lock variants if you want a typewriter. Left variant is a properly integrated geometric swap `[]` on the bracket keys, **Ctrl+X V Z C** packed). Vim/emacs still oriented.

**Why HackHaste is best:** It fits *English’s* split (breath vs stop) and *hacking’s* split (thought-symbols vs clusters), instead of attempting to simply equalize finger counts.

**In the tradition of** **Dr. August Dvorak**, an educational psychologist, vowel blocks are kept distinct (`UIAEO` & `OEAIUY` vs `AOEUI`). HaHa was designed by Dr. Marcus Roe around a **philosophy of the syllable**. No other keyboard layout uses such a philosopher’s principle of language patterns.
